# Attribution and project notes

The collection viewer is derived from VIKUS Viewer by Christopher Pietsch and contributors, used under the MIT License included in `LICENSE.md`.

Collection metadata, research interpretation and the curated selection are based on Shuyan Zhang's 2025 master's thesis, *Family Memory and Literati Networks: The Artistic Value and Cultural Transmission of the Qingshan Hall Stone Inscriptions*.

The prototype separates three kinds of statements:

1. thesis-supported facts;
2. provisional dates or script labels marked for review;
3. AI-assisted first-pass visual descriptions, always followed by human correction.

Original rubbing scans remain the author's research material and are not licensed by the upstream VIKUS Viewer license.
